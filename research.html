Research

Our group works on a broad range of problems in dispersive partial differential equations and related areas of analysis. We are particularly interested in the qualitative behavior of solutions to nonlinear evolution equations and in the analytical structures underlying dispersive phenomena.

Research Directions

Nonlinear Dispersive Equations

We study nonlinear dispersive equations arising in mathematical physics, including nonlinear Schrödinger equations, KdV-type equations, and related models. Topics of interest include well-posedness, long-time behavior, scattering, and the formation of singularities.

Hamiltonian PDEs and Normal Forms

We are interested in Hamiltonian structures of nonlinear evolution equations, normal form transformations, resonant interactions, and their applications to the qualitative and long-time behavior of solutions.

Solitons and Coherent Structures

We investigate solitary waves and other coherent structures, including questions concerning their existence, stability, interactions, and asymptotic dynamics.

Scattering and Long-Time Dynamics

We study the long-time behavior of solutions to nonlinear dispersive and wave equations, including scattering, modified scattering, asymptotic behavior, and nonlinear interactions occurring over large time scales.

Dispersive and Harmonic Analysis

Techniques from Fourier and harmonic analysis play an important role in our research. We are interested in analytical tools and estimates arising in the study of nonlinear dispersive equations, including dispersive, multilinear, and space-time estimates.
