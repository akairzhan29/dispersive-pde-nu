Dispersive PDE Research Group

Mathematics, School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence
Nazarbayev University, Kazakhstan

We are a research group in the Department of Mathematics at Nazarbayev University with a shared interest in dispersive partial differential equations and related areas of analysis. Our group brings together faculty members, postdoctoral researchers, and students working on mathematical problems arising in the study of nonlinear dispersive and wave phenomena. We aim to foster collaboration and interaction through research projects, seminars, working groups, and research visits, while providing an active and welcoming environment for researchers and students interested in dispersive PDEs and related topics.
