Opportunities

We welcome inquiries from researchers and students interested in dispersive partial differential equations and related areas of analysis. Opportunities for collaboration, research visits, graduate study, and postdoctoral research may be available through members of the group.

Postdoctoral Positions

Information about available postdoctoral positions will be posted here. Researchers interested in possible future opportunities are welcome to contact members of the group whose research interests are closely related to their own.

PhD Students

Prospective PhD students interested in dispersive PDEs and related areas are encouraged to contact potential supervisors among the group members. Please consult the university's official graduate admissions pages for information about admission requirements, application procedures, and funding opportunities.

Visitors and Collaboration

We welcome interaction with researchers working in dispersive PDEs and neighboring areas. Researchers interested in visiting the group, developing collaborations, or participating in our research activities are encouraged to contact a relevant group member.
